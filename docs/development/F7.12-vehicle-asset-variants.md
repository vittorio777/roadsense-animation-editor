# F7.12 Vehicle Asset Variants

**Status:** Completed in the original MVP development record.

## Requirement and scope

Add two final independently calibrated vehicle models through the existing generic authoring/preview/persistence chain, demonstrating D002 in production.

## Design and implementation

Register car-blue-sedan/Car Blue Sedan and car-blue-sport/Car Blue Sport at 120 × 72, +X with transparent SVGs. Fix invalid colors/export residue/static bright lamps without redesigning bodies. Each definition has its own eight calibrated anchors/radii/beam values. Replace historicalcar-blue entry through normal registry semantics without alias/deny-list/migration. Generic rendering never reads SVG element IDs or branches by model. F7.13 verifies scale; F7.14 verifies complete workflow.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC1:** Exactly two registered loadable valid model definitions, no old third entry.
2. **AC2:** All identity lookup follows registry with generic unknown behavior, no historical-string special case.
3. **AC3:** Library/Add/counts/properties use independent thumbnails/names/assets.
4. **AC4:** Independent finite positive model geometry without proportional guesses/shared fallback.
5. **AC5:** Inactive models have clear direction and nonactive lamps with intact SVG.
6. **AC6:** Travel-side indicator/hazard maps to calibrated lamps through rotations.
7. **AC7:** Brake lamps are at each model's rear.
8. **AC8:** Headlights/beams originate at front and rotate; inactive has no beams.
9. **AC9:** Mixed movement/states/selection/seek/play/pause/drag stay independent.
10. **AC10:** Save/load retains both IDs/data/visual behavior and rejects unknown normally.
11. **AC11:** Desktop/fallback images/names/handles/events remain usable.
12. **AC12:** No third model/upload/asset switching/migration/new state/SVG protocol/branches.
13. **AC13:** Focused/regression/full checks pass.

## Verification and acceptance

Full 70 files/497 tests, strict type checking, production build and diff checks passed; existing advisory only. Browser displayed both models with independent names/orientation/lights and no console warnings/errors. Final human acceptance 2026-10-02 covered calibrated models, mixed independence and existing workflows.
