# F3.2 — Playhead & Current Time

**Status:** Completed in the original MVP development record.

## Requirement and scope

Click the ruler or blank track surface to set temporary currentTime, with a synchronized playhead and readout. This feature did not snap, drag, interpolate vehicle motion or play continuously.

## Design and implementation

Map time = start + pointerX/surfaceWidth × (end-start), clamp to 0–10, and share ruler/track surface. Display two decimals without changing stored precision. A high-contrast line/marker uses the ruler's percentage coordinates, stays within endpoints and does not intercept events or focus. Timeline subscribes only to currentTime/setCurrentTime.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC1:** Initially show playhead at zero and readout 0.00 s.
2. **AC2:** Ruler/track clicks update time linearly across the surface.
3. **AC3:** Start/midpoint/end yield 0 / 5 / 10 seconds and 0 / 50 / 100 percent.
4. **AC4:** Retain unsnapped precision between ticks.
5. **AC5:** External store time changes and clicks synchronize both visual displays.
6. **AC6:** Clamp slight measurement/pointer overshoot to the range.
7. **AC7:** Seeking preserves project reference/content and movement identity/count/coordinates.
8. **AC8:** Seeking preserves selected vehicle and its properties.
9. **AC9:** Name the click surface and expose readable time without redundant decorative focus.
10. **AC10:** Keep stable layout and complete endpoint markers across desktop/fallback sizes.
11. **AC11:** No snapping, point tracks, interpolation, play/pause or drag interaction.
12. **AC12:** Mapping, clamps, synchronization, data/selection protection and layout regressions pass with full checks.
